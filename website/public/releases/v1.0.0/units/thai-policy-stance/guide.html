Slide 3
state reserve stance
make clear that Thailand has not announced a Bitcoin reserve policy
Sources
BOT - Currency Reserves
https://www.bot.or.th/en/our-roles/banknotes/bmd-page/manage-note.html
Accessed: 2026-07-29

Official BOT page listing lawful components of Thailand currency reserves, such as gold, foreign currencies, foreign securities, IMF assets, and government securities; Bitcoin is not listed.

BOT - International Reserves statistics
https://app.bot.or.th/BTWS_STAT/statistics/ReportPage.aspx?language=eng&reportID=80
Accessed: 2026-07-29

Official BOT reserve statistics showing reported reserve categories such as gold, SDRs, IMF reserve position, and foreign currency reserves. This supports the inference that no public Bitcoin reserve position has been announced by BOT as of 29 July 2026.
